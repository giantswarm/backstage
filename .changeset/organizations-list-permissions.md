The organizations come from agent-manager's `list_organizations` (agent-manager 2.24.0 or later), which replaces `list_my_organizations`. The Organizations page also lists the organizations the person manages without belonging to them. A member sees an organization's status without its details. Adding or removing members, adding a model and replacing a key or a credential are offered only to people the cluster allows to make those changes in the organization namespace.
