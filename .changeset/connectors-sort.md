The Connectors tab of the Customize screen lists every connector at once, by display name (else by name, whatever the case), with muster and the platform's own connectors among the rest. A click on the Connector, Sign-in or Tools header sorts by that column, on the Status header by urgency (Sign in needed first, healthy connectors last); a second click reverses it.
