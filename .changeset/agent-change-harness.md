Agents: the Agent actions menu on an agent's page offers Change Harness. Its dialog lists the runtimes of the agent's namespace and the checks of the one you pick, blocks the change when a check fails, and links to the edit page to fix it. Running sessions stay on the runtime they started on until you delete them; on the agent's Sessions tab they are marked Earlier revision.
