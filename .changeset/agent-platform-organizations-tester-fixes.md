Organizations page: an installation whose check failed shows once, in the list of installations that could not be checked, and no longer also as a warning with agent-manager's raw error. In the Tools step, the organization's connectors section says that an admin of the organization adds a connector through an agent, that a missing credential is set with Replace credential on the Connectors tab, and that "Nothing outside the chat" turns these connectors off too.
