Under the `agent-platform-shell` flag, the Sessions page is titled "Sessions" with a "New session" button, and its list shows one table per day the sessions started (Today, Yesterday, Earlier), states in the shell's words (Waiting for you, Working, Finished, Failed) with a coloured dot, state chips with counts, an agent filter, search by title or agent, and a menu per row to rename or delete the session. Without the flag the Sessions page is unchanged.
