AI Chat displays muster URL elicitations with the requesting server, destination, full HTTPS URL and warnings for internationalized hosts. People can open the page, continue, decline, cancel or stop waiting for a pending retry; leaving the prompt aborts its request. Tool results preserve structured content and native media. gs-node adds `callToolResult`, gs-common adds `toolInputRequiredOf`, and ui-react exports URL descriptions and warning text plus an external-link press handler.
