A workspace has its own page, at
`/customize/workspaces/<installation>/<organization>/<name>`: its sources, the
last sync with its result (a failure in the workspace-manager's words) and
what started it, what the volume stores and its size, the repositories of the
last sync with their head commits and those pushed to since, and the Sessions
on it with their cleanup dates. **Sync now** shows the sync running and reports
its result when it ends. Edit and Delete sit beside it. The Workspaces tab's
names and Open, and a Session's workspace drawer, link to it; a person outside
the workspace's Organization gets a "not a member" page.
