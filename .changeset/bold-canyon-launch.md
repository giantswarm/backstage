The new-session composer keeps the agent and workspace readable however many toolchains are picked. The toolchains button shows a toolbox icon and the number picked, and lists them in its tooltip and popover. The workspace picker shows a folder icon instead of a "Workspace" label, and no longer says "Last used with …" or "…'s default" beside it. A long workspace name ends in an ellipsis, while the agent name keeps its width.
