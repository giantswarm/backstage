Notification inbox: its toasts go on their own after 6 seconds instead of
staying until closed — the catch-up summary, a burst, a session waiting,
failing or finishing its turn, an MCP server waiting for sign-in, and a
notification from another portal feature. The bell keeps the row. A toast
still closes sooner once its row is read or its page is opened, and stays while
the pointer rests on it.
