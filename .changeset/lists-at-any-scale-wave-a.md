Long lists stay light, short lists stay bare. A session's conversation renders its newest 20 turns, with _Show earlier activity_ above them adding 20 at a time, and a folded "Used N tools" run is no longer kept on the page until it is opened. Customize → Skills shows 24 skill cards with _Show 24 more skills_, and its "most used first" note appears only when the catalog is longer than that. All Sessions shows its search, agent picker and state chips only above 8 sessions (the agent picker only with two or more agents), and _Load more_ keeps at most 100 sessions on the page, with _Show previous_ to go back.
