Owner (formerly Organization) keeps its radio group, Platform or Organization, with Platform preselected; choosing Organization shows the organization dropdown, one always picked. A sole environment or owner is a line, not a control; a portal's only environment is named first under More options. Continue waits until the owner is known, and says why while it is read or could not be. The agents list starts the form's agent-manager reads for the likely environment, so Owner no longer arrives late. The agent's page and the agents list's filter and rows say Owner too, and name the platform's agents "Platform" instead of "None".
