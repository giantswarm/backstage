Sessions and the new-session home work on a phone. All Sessions, and an agent's Sessions tab, list stacked rows at phone width instead of a table that left the title ~31px: the title (the link, wrapping to two lines) with its pin and ⋯ menu, then the agent, state, environment and start time under it; the agent's tab shows a page of rows at a time instead of the table's paging. On touch input Enter breaks the line in the session composer, the home composer and a question's answer box, and the Send button sends; the captions say "Tap …" instead of naming Shift+Enter, and the soft keyboard's Enter key is labelled to match. While the agent waits on a question or a permission, the disabled message box folds to its one-line caption (the cost chip stays beside it), and the card's proposed arguments scroll with the card instead of in a box of their own, so the conversation keeps its room. The session's docked composer publishes its height, so a toast lands above it. Tool payloads and reasoning scroll in boxes no taller than two fifths of the view. The command palette's agent rows carry a "Start a session" button on touch, and its keyboard hints are left out there; the new-session dialog is full screen on a phone. The home page no longer scrolls by its own bottom margin, the Slack banner leaves out its decorative thread on a phone, a long email wraps on the profile, and the shell's sign-in page fills the dynamic viewport.
