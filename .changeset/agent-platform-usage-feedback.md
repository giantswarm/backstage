Usage screen: follow the platform's content style and keep every view useful. All four views (and the second-level tab strip) sit in the platform's centered content column (the ShellPage max-width idiom) instead of filling the whole content area. On an installation without gateway (gen_ai) metrics (`mimirEnabled: false`), **Overview** shows the kagent-derived tiles — your sessions, your tokens, the runtime-reported cost where any turn reported one, and your agents — under a note that fleet-wide token and cost figures need the gateway metrics, instead of a mostly-empty page; **Cost** keeps its compact honest empty state and adds the kagent-reported cost when one exists. Nothing is invented: no estimated spend without a gateway-derived rate, and "not reported" never renders as `$0.00`. The 7/30/90-day control now applies to **Your sessions** too: the kagent fan-out's window is fixed (a backend caching contract), so a narrower selection is cut client-side from the daily buckets — turns and tokens re-total, while sessions, tool calls and the reported cost read `—` with a hint saying they exist only over the full window, and the tables name the window they cover; a wider selection says it shows the backend's own window rather than claiming days it never read. On Your sessions, window options wider than the window kagent reports are greyed out and unselectable, with a tooltip naming how many days kagent keeps; a wider window reached by link or tab switch still renders with that explanatory note.
