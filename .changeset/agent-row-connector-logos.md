Customize → Agents shows each agent's connectors on its row as overlapping round logos, at most three with "+N" for the rest, each named on hover or focus; none for full access, as on the agent page. The muster plugin exports `ConnectorLogoStack`, and `ConnectorLogo` takes `round`.
